What Nowvelope does with your data

Last updated 17 September 2026

The short version: your captures stay on your device. There is no account, no Nowvelope server, and no tracking.

What we collect

Nothing. Nowvelope has no account system and no backend of its own. We don't collect, transmit, store on a server, sell, or share your photos, panoramas, audio, or usage. There's no analytics or advertising code, and the app links no networking of its own.

Where your panoramas live

Everything a capture produces — source photos, the stitched panorama, its thumbnail, any optional audio, and its private naming and location details — is stored on your device, in the app's own storage. Those files may be included in your ordinary device backups according to your iOS and backup settings; the app doesn't separately upload or sync them. Delete a panorama and its owned files and details are deleted with it.

Place naming — the one thing that uses the network

In plain terms

Place-name lookup is the only thing Nowvelope sends off your device on its own: a single coordinate — latitude, longitude, altitude, accuracy, and time — to Apple's geocoder, which returns a place name. Nothing else leaves your device unless you send it: your photos, panoramas, and audio go only where you choose to share or export them, never to Nowvelope. Scene names come from models running on your device. Nowvelope has no server of its own.

This happens only if you turn location on and enable place naming. What Apple's map service receives is a coordinate, not your picture, and it's used to look up a concise place name — a landmark, neighborhood, or city — that's then stored privately with your panorama. The request is brief and cancelled after a few seconds; Nowvelope keeps only that one short name, never a street address, postal code, or contact details. Capture and saving work normally with location off.

Permissions, and what happens if you decline

  • Camera — the core function. Asked for when you start a capture, not at launch.
  • Location (optional) — asked for only after you turn on Add Location to Panoramas and accept an in-app explanation. Used only to name and privately tag a panorama with where it was taken; the app never asks for background or “Always” location. Decline and capture still works, just without a place name.
  • Microphone (optional) — asked for only after you turn on Record Ambient Audio and confirm an explanation. Records environmental sound only during an active capture, stored on your device, played back with that panorama. No speech recognition, no transcription, no background recording. Decline and you get a normal, camera-only panorama.
  • Photos — add-only, asked for only when you choose Save to Photos. Nowvelope adds a panorama to your library; it doesn't read your library.

Sharing and export

Sharing is always something you start. Ordinary image sharing and Save to Photos export the final panorama only, rebuilt without embedded GPS. A separate, explicitly-confirmed Export Capture Data action makes a standard archive that can include exact location, audio, and precise capture facts — the confirmation says so plainly, and it only happens when you ask for it and choose where it goes.

Children

Nowvelope is a general-audience camera app. It doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, because it doesn't collect personal information at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the “last updated” date above changes with it. Because the app's behavior and this page are kept in step, a change here reflects a real change in the app.
