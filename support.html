Help with Nowvelope

If something isn't working — a capture that won't finish, guidance that's unclear, playback or export trouble — I'd like to help.

Getting a clean capture

Hold your iPhone upright in portrait, keep it steady while the sphere sets up, then follow the dots to each direction. Even light and a little overlap between directions give the best results. If a spot goes wrong, you can recapture just that direction.

Interruptions

Taking a call, switching apps, or locking the screen mid-capture won't lose your progress — the capture is on the phone the whole time, and it picks back up where you left off.

Sending a diagnostics report

If a capture won't finish or the app gets stuck, the quickest way to get it looked at is a diagnostics report. Open the About screen and choose to share it — you see exactly what it contains before anything leaves your phone: recent app events, error codes, the current storage and capture state, and your app version, device model, and iOS version. It never includes your photos, panoramas, audio, or location.

Nothing is sent on its own. Once you've read it, share it however you like — emailing it to nowvelope@gmail.com is the most direct. Nowvelope has no server of its own, so a report only reaches me because you chose to send it.

Contact

Email nowvelope@gmail.com. For a bug, the diagnostics report above tells me the most; otherwise a note about your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you were doing helps a lot.
