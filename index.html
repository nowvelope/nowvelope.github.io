A 360° camera for iPhone that keeps your pictures to itself.

Two captures — a mountain summit, a temple at first light. Shot on iPhone · stitched on the phone.

Point your phone around, follow the dots to each direction, and Nowvelope stitches the whole sphere into one picture you can step back inside and look around in. All of it happens on the phone.

It stays with you

Most 360° apps send your frames to a server to stitch them. This one doesn't. There's no account and no Nowvelope server, so nothing you shoot — no photo, no panorama, no sound — is ever uploaded anywhere.

The one thing that ever touches the network is optional and yours: turn on place naming and the phone asks Apple's map service for the name of where you are — just a coordinate, never your picture. The full account is here.

A few things it does

  • Records the room, if you want. Switch on ambient audio before a capture and the sound of the place — the street, the weather — plays back with it.
  • Keeps people whole. When someone walks through the shot, it handles them without tearing anyone in half at a seam or leaving an accidental twin.
  • Works with no signal. Since there's nothing to upload, a capture finishes the same on a subway platform as at home.
