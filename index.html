The best camera is the one you have with you.

Nowvelope is a 360° camera for iPhone that keeps your pictures to itself.

Point your phone around, follow the dots to each direction, and Nowvelope stitches the whole sphere into one picture you can step back inside and look around in. All of it happens on the phone.

What sets it apart

  • Sound of the place

    Switch on ambient audio before a capture and the street, the weather, the room play back with the panorama.

  • People kept whole

    When someone walks through the shot, Nowvelope spots them — no one torn in half at a seam, no accidental twin.

  • Stitched on your phone

    No account, no Nowvelope server, no signal needed. Your photographs never leave your phone unless you share or export them.

The one thing it sends on its own is optional: turn on place naming and the phone asks Apple's map service for the name of where you are — just a coordinate, never your picture. The full account is here.
